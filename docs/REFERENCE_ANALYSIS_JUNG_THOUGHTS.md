## Why this video got 1.78M while others get 3k

Factors visible in the data and metadata:

1. **Title/angle**: «Stop Trying to Be Understood» is a personal, painful, universally relatable promise — not «Jung philosophy lecture».
2. **First 5 seconds**: strong B&W cinematic hook image + calm voice.
3. **Education category + Jung/psychology tags** → high-RPM educational audience.
4. **Evergreen search**: Carl Jung is a high-demand search topic; this video keeps accumulating views over months.
5. **Channel authority**: 212 videos and a large back catalog; YouTube trusts the channel.
6. **Outlier dynamics**: philosophy rarely goes viral; when it does, it is usually one strong emotional angle + search + back-catalog trust.

## What to copy (and what not)

Copy:

- emotional, second-person titles («Stop Trying to Be Understood», «You've Changed — Act Like It»)
- strong cinematic first image
- slow Ken Burns over curated images
- calm voice + ambient music
- Education category, psychology/philosophy tags
- 12–25 min length with mid-roll ads
- reuse a small curated image pool across many videos

Do not copy:

- specific images or paintings (copyright/public domain status varies; use public-domain or AI-generated)
- the exact script
- the assumption that every video will get 1M views

## Implication for our pipeline

Our AI-documentary plan already matches the mechanical part:
- scenario → images → local TTS → Ken Burns + crossfade + music → long video.

The missing part is **selection and packaging**:
- generate many title/hook variants and pick the strongest emotional angle
- use a strong first image selected specifically for the hook
- keep the visual pool small and consistent but varied
- publish enough videos to let 1 outlier pay for the rest
